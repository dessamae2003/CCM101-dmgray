# Checkpoint 8 – Mission Reflection

Throughout this laboratory activity, exploring Amazon Web Services, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform provided deep insight into how modern multi-cloud infrastructure operates. Among the three major platforms, Google Cloud Platform impressed me the most due to its pioneering leadership in container orchestration with Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) and its powerful data analytics capabilities. 

Despite their distinct market branding, striking similarities exist among AWS, Azure, and GCP. All three providers rely on globally distributed regions and availability zones, employ robust virtualization layers for compute resources, enforce strict access control via Identity and Access Management (IAM), and offer scalable object storage solutions. 

However, selecting a cloud provider is fundamentally driven by business requirements rather than technical preference alone. A startup prioritizes cost efficiency and rapid scalability, a corporate enterprise often leans toward hybrid-cloud capabilities or existing vendor agreements, a Microsoft-centric environment requires seamless Active Directory integration, and AI research firms demand specialized TPU/GPU hardware. Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that architectural decisions must always align with a client's specific budget, existing infrastructure, and performance goals. 

Finally, completing this mission has significantly improved my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio. Packaging structured research reports, side-by-side comparison tables, multi-cloud decision matrices, and Linux terminal investigations into a professional repository has greatly enhanced my technical documentation skills and my readiness to work as a cloud professional.
